## What changed

same claim. The Latin run still ends on a Persian word, so the reshape does not
undo what U4 did — it reaches the same place without adding meaning.

## Notes

**Why this wording and not the alternative.** The plan offered two: add the
clarification to `en`/`ru`/`zh`, or reshape the Persian. The second is what
`Files` permits, and the first would have been a scope crossing needing the
owner's word. It is also the better of the two on the merits — `در خود پروتکل`
glosses a term `CONTEXT.md` already defines, so the other three locales are not
missing information a reader needs, they are simply not repeating the glossary
in an error message.

**What would reopen this.** If a user report shows readers of any locale take
"VLESS Encryption" for transport security, the fix is to add the clarification
to all four, not to one.
