## What changed

`CLAUDE.md` no longer tells every agent session the opposite of the truth. It
said tests do not gate a merge; they do — `ci.yml`'s `test` job runs the suite on
every pull request and a `master` ruleset requires that check. It now names
`ci.yml`, says plainly that a red suite blocks the merge, demotes `deploy.yml` to
the Pages-only job it is, and names the four gates and the three `src/meta/`
suites the old text did not know existed.
